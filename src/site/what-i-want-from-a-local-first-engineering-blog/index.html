What I want from a local-first engineering blog

September 3, 2026

I want a place for engineering notes that does not become another product to operate. Writing should begin with a file, and reading should end with a page that asks nothing from its visitor.

A quiet default

The first version needs only the pieces that help a reader: text, useful links, and images when an image adds something. It does not need an account, a tracking pixel, or a bundle of scripts.

  • Plain HTML and CSS that work without JavaScript.
  • Local font files and locally served images.
  • A legible layout at a wide desk or a narrow phone.

Constraints are a way of keeping the surface area small enough to care for.

Make room for the actual note

Typography should support the writing rather than compete with it. A chunky heading and a short line of colour are enough to give the page its own character.

<h1>A useful engineering note</h1>
<p>Published as a static page.</p>

The next step is not more machinery1. It is publishing another useful note. The companion note on a small deployment pipeline gives that publishing path a clear shape.
