TinkerQuest - Nightshift and an MVP

August 30, 2026

It is Sunday; I have time at my disposal and I start working on implementing TinkerQuest. Unexpectedly, I end up in the waiting area of our hospital. Luckily, it is nothing serious, but now I have a lot of spare time at my disposal and it really starts to pay off that I went mobile-first. I sit in my uncomfortable chair for hours and build with Codex. At roughly 3am Monday morning, the first MVP is ready.

Time to catch some sleep.
